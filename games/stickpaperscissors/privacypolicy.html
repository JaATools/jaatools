Stick Paper Scissors – Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Stick Paper Scissors is a game made by jaa. This page explains what the game does with information about you. In short: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

No account, no data collected

You play without signing in or creating an account. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information, and this version does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (levels, coins, skins, scores, puzzle stars and options) are saved in the game's own storage on your device. They never leave it, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game.

Songs you choose

If you pick a song from your device to fight to, the game reads that file to find its beat and keeps what it learned on your device. The song is not uploaded or shared.

Clips you save

When you press SHARE CLIP, the game saves a short animated picture of your best move to the Download folder on your device (in "Stick Paper Scissors"). Nothing is uploaded. You decide whether to share it, and with whom, using your own apps.

Fight codes

Fight and challenge codes are short pieces of text that describe a fight (the hands played and the fighters' looks). They contain no personal information, and they are only shared if you copy and send them yourself.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for the little buzz when hits land. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Ads and purchases in future versions

A future version may offer optional rewarded ads (from Google AdMob) and a one-time purchase through Google Play. Before any such version is released, this policy will be updated to explain what information those services use and how you can control it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
